FAQ

Common Questions

Everything small businesses in Belgium want to know before getting started.

How much does a professional website cost for a small business in Belgium?

Every project is different — the price depends on the number of pages, the features you need (like booking systems or contact forms), and the level of development required. We always provide a clear, custom quote before starting — no hidden fees.

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard website takes 2 to 4 weeks from start to launch. More complex projects with a content management system (CMS) or custom features can take 4 to 8 weeks. We keep you updated throughout the entire process.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

SEO grows your organic search visibility over time — it helps potential customers find you on Google without paying per click. Google Ads (pay-per-click / PPC) gives you instant visibility, and you only pay when someone actually clicks your ad. Both are valid strategies and we offer both. We will advise which fits your goals best.

Do I need web hosting and a domain name?

Yes — every website needs web hosting and a domain name. We offer managed hosting plans including daily backups, SSL, and 24/7 monitoring. We can also help you register your domain and get everything set up.

What is GEO and why should my business care?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization — it means optimizing your business to be recommended by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Claude. As more people search through AI instead of Google, GEO is becoming as important as SEO. Morfye is one of the first agencies in Belgium offering this service.
