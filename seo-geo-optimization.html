What is SEO and Why Does It Matter?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the practice of optimizing your website to rank higher in results across Google, Bing, and other search engines. When people search for services you offer, your SEO ranking determines whether you appear on page 1 or buried on page 10.

Here's the harsh truth: 75% of people never scroll past the first page of Google results. If your website isn't ranking well, you're invisible to potential customers actively searching for what you offer.

Good SEO doesn't just bring more traffic — it improves your conversion rate by attracting targeted visitors ready to buy. It's one of the most cost-effective marketing strategies available, because unlike paid ads you're not paying for every click. Traffic from organic SEO compounds over time.

At Morfye, we combine traditional SEO with modern GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) to ensure you're visible everywhere your customers are looking.

What is GEO?

Online visibility has changed dramatically — organic search traffic from SERPs has shifted as traditional clicks from search engine results have fallen by over 55% since 2022. Searchers now ask AI assistants instead of clicking through search engine rankings. Businesses relying only on Google search rankings are losing clients without even realizing it.

GEO is the evolution of optimizing for search in the AI era. While SEO focuses on ranking factors like title tags, meta descriptions, keywords, and backlinks to improve your Google ranking — GEO ensures AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google Gemini recommend YOUR business when people ask for help.

More people are now asking AI assistants instead of Google. If your content isn't optimized for AI indexing, you're invisible to this growing audience — even if your SEO ranking and organic traffic are strong.

Improve your competitive position now — GEO is not just the future, it's happening today. Businesses that adapt early see more conversions while competitors wonder where their clients went.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term strategy. You'll typically see initial improvements in 3–6 months, with significant ranking gains after 6–12 months. Unlike PPC (pay-per-click), organic search results from SEO compound over time — you're not paying for every click.

What are the most important SEO tactics?

An effective SEO strategy uses: keyword research to find what your customers search for; on-page SEO (title tags, meta descriptions, URLs); technical SEO to ensure your pages are indexed correctly; link building to earn high-quality backlinks; content marketing to target long-tail keywords; and local SEO for geographic targeting.

What is the difference between on-page and off-page SEO?

On-page SEO covers what's on your web pages — title tags, headings, URLs, meta tags, keyword phrases, internal linking, and content quality. Off-page SEO is about your authority across the web — backlinks, inbound links, and external mentions. Both are essential for strong search engine rankings.

What SEO tools do you use?

We use Google Search Console, Google Analytics, SEMrush, and Ahrefs to track search queries, monitor backlinks, analyze competitors, and measure your SEO performance. We provide monthly reports so you always know where you rank.

What's the difference between SEO and paid ads (Google Ads)?

Paid search (Google Ads, PPC) gives instant visibility — you pay per click and appear at the top of results immediately. Organic search from SEO takes longer but delivers lasting, cost-effective results without paying per click. Ideally, use paid search for quick wins while SEO builds your long-term foundation.

Do you guarantee #1 rankings on Google?

No ethical SEO company can guarantee specific rankings — Google's algorithm is too complex. We focus on data-driven techniques that consistently improve search rankings, grow organic traffic, and deliver measurable ROI. We track and report every improvement.

What is the difference between white-hat and black-hat SEO?

White-hat SEO follows search engine guidelines — quality content, legitimate link building, proper technical optimization. Black-hat SEO uses manipulative tactics like keyword stuffing and link schemes. While black-hat tactics can show short-term gains, they risk Google penalties. We exclusively use white-hat techniques.

Is local SEO different from regular SEO?

Yes. Local SEO focuses on ranking in specific geographic areas (like Brussels or Belgium) for location-based queries. It includes optimizing your Google Business Profile, building local citations, and targeting location-specific keywords. It's one of the most cost-effective strategies for small businesses.

How much does SEO cost?

It varies based on competition, your niche, and scope. We provide custom quotes tailored to your goals after an SEO audit.

What happens if I stop SEO services?

Your rankings won't disappear overnight, but competitors who keep optimizing will eventually outrank you. SEO requires ongoing content marketing, link building, and technical maintenance to stay ahead as algorithms evolve.

How long does GEO take to show results?

Initial improvements can be seen in 2–4 weeks. Full optimization typically takes 2–3 months as AI engines index and understand your updated content, structured data, and inbound authority signals.

How is GEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO helps your web pages rank in organic results on Google through on-page SEO (title tags, meta descriptions, headers), technical work (crawl optimization, mobile-friendly design), and off-page signals (backlinks). GEO targets AI assistants directly — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity — which don't use the same ranking factors as Google. A complete digital marketing strategy needs both.

Does GEO help with local search?

Yes — local GEO is one of the most powerful tactics for local businesses. When searchers ask AI 'best web designer near me' or 'accountant in Brussels,' you want to be the answer. We optimize your local presence so AI engines know your niche, location, and services.

What is the role of content marketing in GEO?

Content marketing is the backbone of GEO. AI engines are trained on text — the more high-quality, relevant content your web pages have, the better AI understands and recommends you. We focus on content that avoids duplicate text, targets long-tail queries, and uses proper meta descriptions and title tags.

Do you build backlinks for GEO?

Yes — citation building, backlinks, and inbound links from authoritative websites are central to our GEO process. AI engines learn from the web — when trusted sources mention your business, you become more credible to AI. This work overlaps with traditional SEO and improves both your search rankings and AI recommendations.

Will GEO replace my current SEO efforts?

No. GEO complements your SEO strategy — you still need traditional SEO for Google rankings, PPC for paid search, and content marketing for inbound traffic. GEO adds a new layer of visibility so that when AI assistants are asked about your niche, your business is recommended.

Which AI engines do you optimize for?

We optimize for ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), Perplexity, Google Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot. Each has different signals — some rely on web crawling, others on their training data — so our tactics are tailored per platform.

Is GEO worth it for local businesses?

Absolutely. Local GEO is incredibly powerful for small businesses. When someone in your niche asks an AI assistant for a recommendation, you want to be the answer for your city or region — whether that's Brussels, Ghent, Antwerp, or beyond.

How much does GEO cost?

It depends on your industry, competition, and goals. We offer both one-time GEO setup and ongoing monthly management — contact us for a custom quote tailored to your business.

What industries benefit most from GEO?

All industries with search queries benefit, but especially: consultants, lawyers, accountants, healthcare providers, real estate agents, local services, and B2B businesses where potential clients use AI to search for and evaluate options before contacting a service provider.
